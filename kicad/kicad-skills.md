# KiCad Skills

Reusable KiCad workflows for this skill collection:

- [Schematic work](kicad-schematic/SKILL.md) — wire, clean up, and verify
  schematics.
- [PCB review](kicad-pcb-review/SKILL.md) — inspect layout, rules, and 3D
  evidence.
- [Gerber review](kicad-gerber-review/SKILL.md) — validate manufacturing files.
- [BOM and part review](kicad-bom/SKILL.md) — resolve component data and
  assembly requirements.
- [Manufacturing export](kicad-export/SKILL.md) — produce reproducible CLI
  outputs and reports.
- [Production packages](kicad-production/SKILL.md) — board-named Gerber ZIPs,
  JLCPCB BOM/CPL files, and complete cleanup of export helpers.

These skills are adapted and neutralized from
[American-Embedded/kistack](https://github.com/American-Embedded/kistack). See
[kicad-third-party-notice.md](kicad-third-party-notice.md) for attribution and
license terms.
